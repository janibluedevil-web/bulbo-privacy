Bulbo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Developer: Bulbo Team · Contact: yousufalam013@gmail.com

Bulbo is a learning game for young children (ages 2–5) about simple circuits, electricity and electrical safety. We built it so that it does not collect personal information from children or parents.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on the device

To work, the app saves a small amount of information only on the device: the first name or nickname a grown-up types for each child profile (optional), the child's age range, which stages are finished, Playground layouts, and settings such as sound, voice and daily play limit. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the full version has been bought on the Google account. Google's handling of payments is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Voice

Spoken instructions use the text-to-speech engine already on the device. The app does not use the microphone or camera and does not record anything.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. It contains no ads, no links out of the app in the children's area, and no in-app purchases outside the grown-ups area, which is protected by a press-and-hold parent gate. Because we collect no personal information, we have no personal information to share, sell or disclose.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
